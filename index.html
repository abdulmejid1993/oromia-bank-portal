About Oromia Bank

Oromia Bank S.C. is an Ethiopian financial institution serving individuals, businesses, farmers, and communities. This portal supports customers visiting the Babile Main Branch with digital queue tickets, service tracking, and loan pre-screening.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I get a queue ticket?

Choose Digital Ticket, enter your name and phone number, select a service, then submit. Keep the ticket number to track your place in the queue.

What does the loan pre-screening do?

It provides an initial estimate based on the information entered. It is not a loan approval; the branch will review any application.

Who can read my contact message?

Contact messages are stored privately and can only be viewed by authorized bank administrators and managers.
